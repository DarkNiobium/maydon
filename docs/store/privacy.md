# MAYDON — Privacy Policy

MAYDON does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- Your profile (player name, number, club, progress, settings) is stored only on your device.
- The game does not contain ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.
- Local multiplayer uses your Wi-Fi network only to connect to friends' devices on the same network;
  no data is sent to any server.
- Network permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE)
  are used solely for local multiplayer discovery and play. VIBRATE is used for button feedback.

Uninstalling the game removes all its data.
