### English
MAYDON is realistic football where you don't control the whole team — you are ONE player.
Every pass, run and shot is your call.

• Floodlit neighbourhood cage: 3v3 and 5v5 with walls and no offside
• Stadium: full 11v11 rules — throw-ins, corners, offside, penalties, cards
• Pick your position: goalkeeper, defender, midfielder or forward
• Smart teammates: ask for the ball with TO ME!, make runs with RUN!
• Tashkent District League: 12 districts — win the title and get promoted to the National League
• Play with friends on the same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot — no internet needed
• Player card, levels, stats, match ratings and Player of the Match
• Uzbek, Russian and English
